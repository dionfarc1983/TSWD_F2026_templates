<h2>Debt-to-GDP Ratio (1995–2019), OECD Countries</h2>

<p>
This highlight table visualizes debt-to-GDP ratios for OECD countries from 1995 to 2019. Countries are sorted by average debt levels, while the diverging color scale highlights values below and above the 100% debt-to-GDP threshold. The visualization makes it easy to compare debt trends across countries and over time.
