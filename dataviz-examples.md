This highlight table shows debt-to-GDP ratios for OECD countries from 1995 to 2019. Countries are sorted by average debt levels, while a diverging color scale centered at 100% highlights values below and above the debt-to-GDP threshold. The visualization enables quick comparison of debt trends across countries and over time.
