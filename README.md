# About me
Hello! My name is AMET Miranda (he/him), and I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Public Policy and Management, Data Analytics (MSPPM-DA) at Carnegie Mellon University. I have a professional background in Industrial Engineering and an MBA, and I bring nearly two decades of experience from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), where I have served in leadership roles related to currency production and manufacturing operations.

Throughout my career, I have been passionate about continuous improvement, process optimization, and evidence-based decision-making. Working in a highly regulated manufacturing environment has taught me the importance of transforming complex operational data into meaningful insights that support better decisions. As a Production Manager, I regularly work with performance metrics, resource planning, quality management, and process improvement initiatives, which have strengthened my appreciation for the power of data when communicated effectively.

Outside of work and academics, I enjoy exploring new technologies, traveling, and learning innovative approaches to organizational improvement. I am particularly interested in how data visualization and storytelling can bridge the gap between technical analysis and practical decision-making. I believe that even the most sophisticated analysis creates value only when it can be clearly understood and acted upon by stakeholders.

# What I hope to learn

Through Telling Stories with Data, I hope to strengthen my ability to communicate analytical findings in a clear, compelling, and audience-centered manner. While my professional experience has provided me with a strong foundation in quantitative analysis and operational reporting, I would like to develop more effective ways to transform data into stories that influence decisions and drive action. Specifically, I am interested in learning best practices in data visualization, design principles, and narrative techniques that make complex information accessible to diverse audiences.

After graduating from Carnegie Mellon University, I plan to return to the Philippines and continue contributing to the public sector through data-driven leadership and organizational improvement initiatives. I hope to leverage the knowledge and skills gained from this program to enhance operational excellence, support strategic decision-making, and promote greater use of analytics within government organizations. Ultimately, I aspire to become a leader who can effectively connect data, people, and policy to create meaningful impact.
